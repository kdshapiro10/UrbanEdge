Overland Park

Luxury Garden-Style Living in Downtown Overland Park

Urban Edge (under construction) will be an exciting new residental development that will feature 36 upscale apartment homes offering a variety of floorplans including one-bedroom, one-bedroom plus den, and two-bedroom options in the heart of Downtown Overland Park, Kansas. Designed for residents seeking both luxury and convenience, Urban Edge blends modern design with private comfort. Each apartment includes assigned covered parking, smart home features, and access to an exclusive suite of amenities shared with our neighboring sister community, Inter Urban Lofts.
